# Surface Brief: Event Planner Main Dashboard

## Scope and Visitor Mode
Scope: Main Event Planning Application Interface (Dashboard, Budget Manager, Run-of-Show Timeline, Vendor Registry, and GenAI Inspiration Workshop).
Mode: operate.

## Audience and Jobs
Event organizers, engaged couples, and community chairs orchestrating complex celebrations under budget constraints and chronological deadlines.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An event planner is an orchestration desk, not a generic card grid. Refuses the repetitive SaaS card-soup and floating gradient widgets in favor of structured financial clarity, a chronological milestone ribbon, and a docked GenAI creative atelier.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight slate primary shell (#0F172A), warm champagne card grounds (#FFFFFF and #FAF8F5), burnished amber-gold accents (#D97706, #B45309), crisp emerald confirmations (#059669), and crimson variance alerts (#DC2626). Tabular lining figures, crisp 1px borders (#E2E8F0), and subtle offset elevation.

STORY: The user immediately sees their celebration pulse: budget variance, upcoming milestones, vendor contract progress, and can invoke Gemini GenAI to expand themes and generate timeline runs.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A top executive event masthead with event switcher, total budget progress bar, and days-to-event countdown; followed by dual-pane layout: on the left, the Chrono-Ribbon timeline and categorized budget balance; on the right, the GenAI idea workshop and active vendor checklist.
